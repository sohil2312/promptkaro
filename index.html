Questions shop owners ask

Is the demo really free?

Yes. We build a working demo first. You pay only if you want the site to go live.

What do I need to send?

Your business name, phone number, address, a few photos and your price list or menu. Whatever you have is enough to start.

Do I have to deal with domain or hosting?

No. We set up the domain and hosting and put the site live for you.

How and when do I pay?

Only after you've seen the demo and decided to go ahead. UPI or bank transfer. The site goes live once payment is complete.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once payment is complete, the design and content are yours.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Every plan includes a set number of change rounds (Starter 1, Business 3, Pro 5). After launch, the care plan (₹1,999 a month) covers regular updates.

Which language can we talk in?

Gujarati, Hindi or English, whichever you are comfortable with.
