# PromptKaro design system

This file is the source of truth for how promptkaro.in looks and moves. It supersedes the visual
direction in `promptkaro_master_prompt.md` and `promptkaro_logo_addendum.md` (the lime-on-black
terminal look). Change this file first, then the CSS.

## The idea

**The WhatsApp chat where every PromptKaro job starts becomes the hero.** Shop owners in
Gujarat already live in WhatsApp, so the page shows the service the way they will actually
experience it: a message, a working demo, "make it live". Everything else is plain and honest:
see it first, pay only if you like it, a quote based on what you need, no invented proof.

- For now the site names no town and shows no phone number (the number lives only inside the
  `wa.me` links, which need it). No delivery-time promise and no fixed prices: every site type
  asks for a quote on WhatsApp.

- Audience: shop owners, clinics, coaching classes, property dealers, restaurants. Mostly on
  phones, often on Jio. Not developers.
- One job: send a WhatsApp message asking for the free demo.
- Examples are full landing pages, not cards: the business picker shows a whole sample site
  (hero, products, about, timings, CTA band) in a browser frame, and pre-fills the WhatsApp
  message with that business type. Sample sites are generated by `tools/samples/build.py`
  (fictional businesses, own fonts) and screenshotted by `tools/samples/shoot.js`.
  **Each sample has its own layout and art direction, never one template recoloured:** a retail
  storefront (offer bar, dark product hero with price and colourways, categories, stock badges,
  open-now card), a clinic (live OPD card with free slots and tokens, priced services list, doctor
  band, WhatsApp booking steps), a coaching class (parent test report, timetable with seats left,
  weekly rhythm, fees), a restaurant (editorial hero, printed-style menu with dotted leaders,
  today's special, reservation card) and a property launch (price tag, key facts, floor plan with
  room sizes, homes left per type, site-visit days). Six more were added later
  (`tools/samples/build_more.py`): an interior and architecture studio (editorial serif, full-bleed
  room photo, project grid), a boutique (Bodoni storefront, try at home), a wedding planner (maroon
  and gold, marigold decor, the wedding week), handicrafts (makers and gifting), furniture (split
  showroom hero, bestsellers, see-it-in-your-room, plus three inner pages for the multi-page
  preview) and a manufacturer (B2B: stats, model table, quote form). No photo repeats more than
  twice on a page. Phones get a phone-width screenshot (`site-<key>-m.webp` via `<picture>`).
- **The examples sit beside each other:** on screens 720px and up the eleven businesses are a
  list down the side (name and a one-line description), the preview fills the middle and "What's
  on this site" sits to its right from 1100px. On phones the list is a row of chips above.
  Generated from `tools/site/showcase.json` by `tools/site/build_showcase.py`.

## Tokens (all in `css/style.css` `:root`)

| Role | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | `#FFFFFF` / `#F4F4EF` | Sections alternate by a tone step, never a line |
| Ink | `#13201A` | Green-black. All running text |
| Ink 2 / 3 | `#3E4943` / `#5B6660` | Secondary / tertiary text, both pass AA |
| Action | `#008069` | WhatsApp's own green. White text 4.9:1. Every CTA |
| Marigold | `#FFB224` | Brand mark: cursor, ₹0, "our pick", focus ring, selection. Never on body text |

**Product cards are the one place the palette widens:** rani pink `#C2185B`, marigold, peacock
`#0F5F66` and neem `#CFE7B8`, cycling so no colour repeats side by side. Each card is a saturated
fill (28px radius, no shadow) holding a white fragment of real UI. The pattern (saturated cards
with live product UI inside) follows the analysis of clay.com in VoltAgent/awesome-design-md; the
colours, fragments, motion and copy are PromptKaro's own, and none of Clay's illustrations or
brand assets are used.

Type: **Anek Gujarati** (Ek Type, Mumbai, OFL), self-hosted, one family for English and
Gujarati. Five sizes: 15 / 18 / 20–24 / 32–52 / 44–108px. Display weight 640, tracking −0.025 to
−0.035em. Gujarati gets more line-height and a smaller hero (`html[lang="gu"]` rules). The
Gujarati font file (159 KB) only downloads when Gujarati text is on screen.

Space: 4px base (`--s-1`…`--s-10`). Radius by nesting: pill for buttons/tabs, 18px cards, 28px
for the chat device. Shadows only on things that float (chat, sample sites, dock).

## Motion (`css/motion.css`)

Based on Emil Kowalski's design-engineering rules.

- Curves: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`,
  `--ease-drawer: cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`. Never `ease-in`, never `transition: all`.
- **The hero stage** (`js/hero.js`, GSAP): a browser, a product card and a WhatsApp phone,
  aligned to the headline's top line. Scene 1 is the PromptKaro story (a shop owner asks on
  WhatsApp and gets a demo). Then it cycles through a furniture store, the automated dashboard, an
  interior studio, a boutique, a manufacturer and a wedding planner: the product card turns away
  and the next one springs in, the next website opens out of the card as a growing circle and
  scrolls itself, and the phone switches to that business's AI agent answering a customer (step
  chip with spinner, typing, reply). Pause button and dots (WCAG 2.2.2); pauses off screen and in
  a hidden tab; reduced motion shows scene 1 complete and the dots switch instantly.
- Page load: hero lines rise 16px with 60–70ms stagger, once.
- Feedback: every pressable scales to 0.97 on `:active` (140ms). Hover effects only under
  `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Previews tour themselves:** once the examples are on screen, the selected preview scrolls
  slowly down its page (about 200px/s, eased), holds, and glides back up, once per preview. Any
  wheel, touch, click or key inside it hands it to the visitor for good. Skipped under reduced motion.
- **Product cards fill themselves in**, once, when 45% visible: the Website card types a search
  then resolves the shop's result and its chips; the Catalog tiles shimmer then fill one by one
  before the share button lands; the **AI shopping assistant** types the customer's own words
  ("Phone under ₹15,000 with a good camera"), works through three agent steps, and lands three
  rendered matches, best first; the Dashboard bars grow left to right as the total counts up.
  Titles and copy are never hidden. (No calculators: an AI that understands a request replaced
  the EMI slider.)
- **Every product ends on WhatsApp:** after its demo, each card hands off: a fingertip taps the
  action, a mini chat opens, the customer's message lands, the shop types and replies. The
  Dashboard shows the owner's side (an enquiry notification; today's pillar and the total tick up).
- **Bot vs AI agent** (top of the agents section): the same free-text message goes to both, played
  side by side. The bot answers from a numbered menu and fails to book; the agent shows its steps
  and books. Labels are distinct everywhere: slate **Bot** tag with a menu glyph, green **AI agent**
  tag with a face glyph. Never call agent replies "auto-reply"; a bot is not an agent.
- **One detailed automated dashboard, not a bar chart:** a full owner's dashboard (sidebar with
  switched-on automations and their run counts, KPIs, revenue through the day against last week,
  sales by channel, latest orders with channel and AI-agent badges, AI insights, stock with an
  automatic reorder, live activity). It rewinds to earlier today, then four orders arrive one by
  one while on screen (new row, feed item, revenue counts up, the earbuds drop to 2 and the
  reorder rule fires) and it stops on the numbers in the HTML.
- **Agent console** (`js/agents.js`): six scripted scenarios (orders, appointments, admissions,
  payments, reviews, leads). The owner's WhatsApp view plays the conversation while the agent log
  streams each step with a spinner, tool chip and time, ending in a summary. Autoplay advances
  every scenario while on screen (Pause / Play all); choosing a tab hands control to the visitor.
- **Site types, quote-based:** one-page, multi-page, with motion and animation, with a catalog,
  with a WhatsApp AI agent, with an automated dashboard, each on a rendered phone or laptop. The
  first three have live screens: the one-page phone scrolls a whole site, the multi-page laptop
  moves through four pages of the furniture site with a page chip following, the motion laptop
  plays a recording. Live content is warped onto the four screen corners measured from each
  render (`data-quad`, a `matrix3d` projection in `js/types.js`), so it sits on the glass at any
  size. Plays only while visible, never under reduced motion.
- **More dashboards:** under the live mobile-shop dashboard, HD dashboards for a furniture store
  (production board), a manufacturer (quote pipeline, dispatch), a wedding planner (the week,
  vendors, RSVPs) and an interior studio (projects, site visits), from `tools/samples/build_dash.py`.
  The agent console has twelve scenarios, including custom sofa sizes, a site visit, try at home,
  a bulk quote with PDF, a wedding enquiry and gift hampers, some with photo cards in the chat.
- **Real product imagery, not vectors:** products (phone, smartwatch, earbuds, charger, three
  phone colourways) and the map pin are physically based 3D renders (original, generic designs) made in
  `tools/render`, shipped as WebP with explicit sizes and lazy loading. On desktop (fine pointer,
  no data saver, motion allowed) a 24-frame turntable sprite swaps in: the product spins into place
  and then follows the mouse across its card. Phones get the light stills (about 20 KB each).
  Sample sites and the hero link preview use the same renders as product photos.
- **Photoreal scenes and products for the newer samples** are rendered with Blender Cycles (bpy,
  path tracing with denoising) by `tools/render/blender/render.py`: rooms with a wood floor, rugs
  and window light; seamless-backdrop product shots; transparent cutouts with real contact
  shadows (shadow catcher), faded at the edges by `export.py`. Furniture, fashion, glass and
  ceramic objects come from the open Khronos glTF Sample Assets (CC0 / CC BY 4.0, listed on
  `/credits.html`, fetched by `fetch-models.sh`); marigold garlands, candles, diyas, terracotta
  pots and the architect's model are modelled in Blender from primitives with PBR materials.
  Never flat vector drawings.
- **Cards feel physical:** on mouse, a product card tilts up to 3° toward the pointer through a
  damped spring (it lags and settles, never snaps) with a soft sheen; on press, mouse or touch,
  it squishes to 0.98.
- Scroll: the "how it works" line fills as you read it (goal gradient). CSS
  scroll-driven only, content never fully hidden.
- Wordmark cursor blinks 3 times on load then holds; hover wakes it. No infinite loops anywhere
  except while something is working (spinners, the live dot).
- **Wordmark caret geometry** (measured on Anek Bold): height 0.68em = cap height, foot on the
  baseline (flex `align-items: baseline`), width 0.1em (thinner than the 0.16em stems, so it reads
  as a caret, not an "l"), gap 0.06em (one letter gap; 0.03em in the tighter footer mark).
- Reduced motion: no travel, fades only; the chat is shown complete and doesn't play.
- Without JavaScript: every message, every sample site and every section is visible.

## Principles, and where they are applied

**Laws of UX**

| Law | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Jakob's law | The chat is styled like real WhatsApp; WhatsApp-green buttons with the real WhatsApp glyph |
| Fitts's law | 48–56px targets; on phones a full-width WhatsApp dock sits in the thumb zone |
| Hick's law | 4 nav links, 6 site types, 5 business types, one action everywhere |
| Von Restorff | Green is used only for "message us" |
| Serial position | The free-demo action opens and closes the page |
| Peak–end | The chat ends on "Make it live 👍"; the page ends on one big CTA and the name |
| Goal gradient | You message → We build a demo → You decide, with a line that fills |
| Doherty threshold | ~41 KB font, no frameworks, no third-party requests; interactions answer in under 300ms |
| Tesler's law | "We handle the domain and hosting"; WhatsApp messages are pre-filled |
| Postel's law | Talk in Gujarati, Hindi or English; the whole site switches EN ⇄ ગુ |
| Cognitive load / chunking | Roughly 80 words per screen; FAQ collapsed; site-type features in short lists |
| Proximity / common region | Picture, description and features grouped per site type; label + value in contact blocks |

**Refactoring UI**

- Hierarchy through weight and colour first, size second: labels small and grey, values large and ink.
- De-emphasise to emphasise: secondary text is `--ink-2`, never a second accent colour.
- A constrained system: 4px spacing scale, 5 type sizes, 3 radii, one shadow.
- Separate with tone and space, not borders.
- Accessible contrast on every pair (checked), no grey text on coloured backgrounds.
- Supercharged defaults: custom tick bullets, tabular numbers on prices, real empty-state-free content.

## Anti-slop audit of the previous site

The previous build came from a prompt that asked for most of the patterns current anti-slop
guides flag. What was there, what replaced it, and why:

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near-black `#0a0a0c` with a single acid-lime accent | White/stone ground, green-black ink, WhatsApp green for action | Black + one neon accent is the second most common generated look; this audience trusts plain, bright, WhatsApp-like UI |
| Clash Display + Satoshi + JetBrains Mono + Rubik, from 3 CDNs | One self-hosted family (Anek Gujarati) | Fontshare default pair reads as template; 4 families on 3 hosts is slow on Jio; Anek covers Gujarati natively |
| Fake macOS terminal with red/yellow/green dots and pixel "build" loop | WhatsApp conversation that shows the real process | Sells to developers, not shop owners; drawn OS windows are a listed tell |
| Rotating word in the headline + one word coloured lime | One sentence, one colour | Styled accent word and word rotators are top generated-headline tells |
| `⚡ AI Vibecoding Studio · 48hr Delivery` pill with pulsing dot | Removed | Eyebrow pill, middle-dot meta string, decorative status dot, jargon |
| Tracked ALL-CAPS tags above every heading | Plain headings that say the thing | Eyebrow labels are template chrome |
| Centred section headers, identical everywhere | Left-aligned, each section a different composition | Everything-centred + same block repeated is the SaaS kit |
| Bento grid of "Coming soon" demos with dead `#` links and grey-bar mockups | Business picker with full-fidelity sample sites and a working CTA | Placeholder dashboards and dead links erode trust |
| `01 > idea_batao` command cards with icon tiles | Three plain steps: You message / We build a demo / You decide | Numbering and icon-topped cards are defaults |
| Emoji as icons (🟢🔵🟣🟡 🛡️ 🙏 🚀) | One SVG glyph set; one 👍 inside the chat where it's content | Emoji-as-icon is a listed tell |
| Count-up stats: "100% Satisfaction", "5+ Projects", "48hr" | Removed | Invented or unverifiable metrics hurt trust more than they help |
| "Real reviews coming soon…" placeholder | Removed; contact details shown plainly | An empty testimonial slot advertises that there are none |
| `→` glued to every button | Removed | Arrow-suffix is template chrome |
| Film grain, drifting dot grid, pulsing glow blobs, bouncing scroll hint | Removed | Endless decorative loops, no purpose |
| Fade-up on every card via JS, content hidden until JS fires | Content visible by default; one orchestrated moment | Fade-up everywhere reads as generated and hid content in screenshots and slow loads |
| `POPULAR` badge + scaled card | "Our pick for most shops" on the one inverted card | Honest recommendation instead of an implied statistic |
| Filler copy ("One Perfect Solution", "Anything You Can Imagine", "AI-First Vibecoding Studio") | Plain, specific copy from the customer's side | Filler verbs and slogans with no content |
| Pulsing floating WhatsApp button + mobile bar + nav button | Nav button + a mobile dock that appears only between the big CTAs | Three competing CTAs at once; pulsing loop |
| `transition` defaults, `ease`, no `:active` states | Custom curves, exact properties, press feedback everywhere | Emil Kowalski's rules |
| Inline styles + `'unsafe-inline'` CSP, third-party font hosts | `style-src 'self'`, hashed inline script, `font-src 'self'` | Tighter security, fewer hosts |

## Layout rules, enforced by tests (section 9)

- **Type scale** (Refactoring UI: a constrained scale): 13 labels, 15 small, 16 UI (buttons, nav,
  tabs), 18 body, 20–24 lead, 24–32 h3, 32–52 h2, the hero h1. Nothing in between.
- **Spacing on a 4px grid:** every margin, padding and gap is a multiple of 4. Section rhythm and
  gutters step at breakpoints (64/96/128 and 16/24/32/40) instead of fluid values.
- **Targets 44px** (Fitts's law / WCAG 2.5.5): links, buttons, tabs, dots, the logo.
- **Nothing is cut:** no ellipsis on real content, tab rows wrap, tables drop columns on phones
  instead of scrolling sideways, captions get their own line.
- **No empty containers:** cards that sit side by side fill with real content rather than
  stretching around a void (the Dashboard card shows today's numbers).
- **Proximity:** headings sit closer to what they introduce than to what came before.
- **Caching:** CSS/JS are linked with `?v=<content hash>` (`tools/site/version.py`, run after any
  CSS/JS change); HTML always revalidates, so a deploy never shows new HTML with old CSS.

## Rules for future changes

1. Every colour and font goes through a token. No new families.
2. No eyebrows, no tracked caps, no middle-dot strings, no arrows on link text, no emoji icons.
3. Never invent a number, testimonial or client logo. Add real ones when they exist.
4. Transform and opacity only; under 300ms for UI; `prefers-reduced-motion` for everything.
5. Content must be visible with JavaScript off.
6. Every new text needs a `data-gu` translation.
7. Run `npx playwright test`; section 8 of the suite lints these rules.

## Resources reviewed

  couldn't be opened from the build sandbox.

  default" look, as the owner's own note says); Lottie / Rive.
- **Used later from the Motion list:** GSAP (self-hosted, `js/vendor/gsap.min.js`, 73 KB, for the
  hero timelines only), Three.js (offline device renders), Blender via `bpy` (the Blender MCP idea
  from the games list, run headless for Cycles renders), FFmpeg (the motion recording).
- **Fonts:** Fontshare, uncut.wtf and freefaces are good free sources, but almost none of them
  cover Gujarati. Anek (Ek Type) does, which is why it was chosen. Fontshare and uncut were
  blocked from the build sandbox, so the sample sites use open fonts from fontsource
  (Instrument Serif, Manrope, Bodoni Moda, Jost, Cormorant, Young Serif, Work Sans, Newsreader,
  Archivo, plus the earlier Bricolage, Fraunces, Figtree, Lexend and Syne).
